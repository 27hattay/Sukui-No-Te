About Us

Who We Are

Sukui No Te (救いの手) — meaning "Helping Hand" in Japanese — is a student-led service group at YIS dedicated to making a positive impact in our community. We believe that small actions, taken together, can create meaningful change.

🤝

Community First

We put people at the heart of everything we do, serving those around us with care and dedication.

🌱

Student Led

Every initiative is driven by students, for students — building leadership through service.

✨

Real Impact

We focus on tangible outcomes and lasting connections within our local and global community.
