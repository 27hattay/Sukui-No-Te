About Us

Who We Are

Sukui No Te (救いの手) — "Helping Hand" in Japanese — is a student-led service group at Yokohama International School dedicated to making a real difference in our community.

We believe that students have the power to create meaningful change. Through hands-on service, fundraising, and outreach, we reach out to those who need support most.
